# Billing and Subscription Plans

NimbusBoard is billed monthly or annually, per workspace. Every workspace starts on a
14-day free trial of the Team plan; no credit card is required until the trial ends.

There are three plans: Starter ($9/user/month), Team ($19/user/month), and Enterprise
(custom pricing, contact sales). Annual billing gets a 20% discount on Starter and Team.
All plans include unlimited projects and unlimited guest viewers; what changes between
plans is the number of full (editing) seats, API rate limits, SSO support, and audit log
retention.

Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and charged automatically
to the payment method on file. You can update your payment method, download past
invoices, or change your plan at any time from Settings > Billing. Downgrading takes
effect at the end of the current billing cycle; upgrading takes effect immediately and is
prorated for the remainder of the cycle.

If a payment fails, NimbusBoard retries it three times over seven days and emails the
workspace's billing admin after each failed attempt. After the third failed retry, the
workspace is moved to a read-only state: no new issues or comments can be created, but
existing data remains fully accessible and exportable until you update payment.
